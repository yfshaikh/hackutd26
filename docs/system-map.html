How your Mac reaches the box

Your Mac and the box are both on your Tailscale network, a private encrypted link. The box's address there is 100.106.83.89. Nothing on the box is open to the public internet.

kubectl and the backend

Talk to the k3s API at 100.106.83.89:6443 using ~/.kube/hackutd.yaml.

The attacker laptop

Reaches the "exposed" Jupyter at 100.106.83.89:8888. For safety, the tailnet stands in for the internet.

SSH (setup only)

Brev's gateway is flaky, so SSH over Tailscale: ssh -o HostName=100.106.83.89 -o Port=22 -o ProxyCommand=none hackutd-gpu
